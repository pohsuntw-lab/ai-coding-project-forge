# Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Last updated / 最後更新：August 30, 2026 / 2026 年 8 月 30 日

## 繁體中文

EW AI Coding 是純技能插件，不營運外部伺服器、不建立獨立使用者帳號，也不自行收集、儲存、出售或分享個人資料。

你提供的內容會依 OpenAI 適用的隱私條款與設定在 ChatGPT 中處理；產生的專案文件由使用者自行管理。

除非確有必要且符合組織規定，請勿提供密碼、API 金鑰、財務資料、醫療紀錄或其他敏感資訊。如有隱私問題，請使用本儲存庫的公開支援管道。

## English

EW AI Coding is a skills-only plugin. It does not operate an external server, create its own user accounts, or independently collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

Content you provide is processed within ChatGPT under OpenAI's applicable privacy terms and settings. Generated project documents remain under the user's control.

Do not include passwords, API keys, financial information, medical records, or other sensitive data unless necessary and permitted by your organization. For privacy questions, use the repository's public support channel.
