# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

AI Coding Project Forge is a skills-only plugin. It does not operate an external server, create its own user accounts, or independently collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

Content you provide is processed within ChatGPT under OpenAI's applicable privacy terms and settings. Generated project documents remain under the user's control.

Do not include passwords, API keys, financial information, medical records, or other sensitive data unless necessary and permitted by your organization.

For privacy questions, use the repository's public support channel.
